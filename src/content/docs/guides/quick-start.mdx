Choose a source, read your first book, and save your place. Mankai supports recommended stream-based plugins and file-based sources for your own books.

If you have not installed Mankai yet, choose a method in [Installation](/guides/installation/).

## 1. Find your way around

Mankai has four main tabs.

| Tab          | What you will find                                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Home**     | Your bookmarked titles and downloaded books            |
| **Library**  | Titles and search provided by your plugins             |
| **Files**    | Local books, connected shares, and file imports        |
| **Settings** | Reader preferences, sources, sync, and reading history |

## 2. Add a source

Choose one of two options:

- **Stream-based (recommended)**: Add a content plugin in **Settings → Plugins** to search and read an external library. Follow the detailed [plugin setup](/guides/sources/#add-a-content-plugin).
- **File-based**: Import book files or connect local and network shares in **Files**. Follow the detailed [file-based source setup](/guides/sources/#file-based-sources).

## 3. Find a book and start reading

1. Open **Library** for plugin titles or **Files** for book files and connected shares.
2. Choose a book from a list or folder. Use search to find a title or author when your plugin supports it.
3. Tap a book to open its details and chapter groups.
4. Tap **Read** to begin. To choose a particular chapter, open a chapter group and tap the chapter you want.
5. Swipe through the pages. Tap near the center of a page to show or hide the reading controls.
6. Use the reader's settings button to choose **Paged** or **Continuous** reading.

Available lists, search, and chapters depend on the source.

## 4. Save the book to your collection

1. Return to the book's details and tap **Bookmark**.
2. Open **Home** and choose **Collections** in the source menu.
3. Open the book again and tap **Continue** to pick up where you left off.

Mankai can automatically check saved titles for new chapters when the plugin supports updates. Pull down on the collection list to check manually. Bookmarking keeps a title in your collection. Download its chapters separately for offline reading when the source supports downloads.
