Open a book from **Files**, **Library**, **Home**, or **Settings → History**. The book details screen shows its description, chapters, and buttons to start or resume reading.

## Start or resume a chapter

1. Tap **Read** to start a book, or **Continue** to pick up where you left off.
2. To choose a specific chapter, open one of the book's chapter groups and tap a chapter.
3. Tap the sort button to reverse the chapter order.

The chapter groups and information shown depend on the book and its source.

:::note[Unavailable chapters]

Locked or unavailable chapters cannot be opened in the reader.

:::

## Move through the book

1. Swipe to turn pages in **Paged** mode, or scroll through pages in **Continuous** mode.
2. Tap near the center of a page to show or hide the controls.
3. Drag the page slider at the bottom to move to another page.
4. Use the previous and next chapter buttons beside the slider to move between available chapters.

The swipe direction and tap behavior depend on your reader preferences.

:::tip[Retry a chapter]

If a chapter fails to load, use the reader's retry action after checking your connection.

:::

## Choose a comfortable layout

Tap the settings button in the reader to adjust the reading layout and controls.

| Setting                       | Use it to                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reader Type**               | Switch between **Paged** and **Continuous**                      |
| **Reverse Reading Direction** | Switch between left-to-right and right-to-left reading           |
| **Tap Navigation**            | Turn pages by tapping page regions                               |
| **Image Layout**              | Choose **Auto**, **One Per Row**, or **Two Per Row**             |
| **Smart Grouping**            | Automatically group pages based on similarities in their content |

Paged mode also offers horizontal or vertical navigation and page transition options.

:::note[Settings availability]

Some settings appear only when they apply to the selected mode or book. If the reader follows the book’s specified direction, its direction control may be disabled.

:::

To set your default preferences, open **Settings → Reader**. You can set the default reader type, decide whether to respect a book's reading direction, and adjust each reading mode. On iPad, try **Two Per Row** when you want paired pages. On iPhone, **One Per Row** gives each page more space.

## Pick up where you left off

Return to the book's details and use **Continue**. You can also find recently read books under **Settings → History**. Add a bookmark to keep a title on **Home** even when it is no longer near the top of your history.
