**Home** brings together your collections and downloads. **Library** is where you discover content offered by your sources.

## Save and find your favorites

1. Open a book's details and tap **Bookmark**.
2. Open **Home** and choose **Collections** in the source menu.
3. Search your saved titles or filter by status and source.
4. Pull down on the collection list to check for updates.

To remove a bookmark, open the book's details and tap **Remove**.

:::note[Bookmarks and downloads]

Removing a bookmark and deleting a downloaded copy are separate actions.

:::

## Find something to read

1. Open **Library** to see the lists offered by your sources.
2. Open a title from a list, or use search if one of your sources supports it.
3. Open the book's details and choose **Read**, **Continue**, or a chapter.

To return to a recently read book, open **Settings → History**. History includes books you have read even if you did not bookmark them.

## Download chapters

1. Open a book from a source that supports downloads and tap its download arrow.
2. In **Select Chapters**, expand a chapter group and select the chapters you want. Tap the circle beside a group to select all of its available chapters.
3. Tap **Download**.
4. Tap the download arrow on **Home** to check progress. A failed task has a retry button.

## Read offline and manage storage

1. Wait for the download to finish while the source is reachable.
2. Choose **Downloads** in the source menu on **Home** and open the book.
3. Open a downloaded chapter to read it offline.

:::note[Downloaded chapters only]

Only the chapters you have downloaded are available offline. Bookmarking a title does not download its pages.

:::

To free storage, swipe a downloaded title in the downloads panel and confirm **Remove**. The download controls also let you cancel pending downloads or retry failed ones.

## Export downloaded chapters

Export selected downloaded chapters as **MMA** or **PDF**:

- **MMA (Mankai Manga Archive)** bundles all selected chapter groups, original page images, book metadata, and cover into one `.mma` file. It usually exports faster than PDF. For archive details, see [MMA format](/api/mma-format/).
- **PDF** creates a separate `.pdf` file for each selected chapter. Selecting three chapters produces three PDF files.

1. Download the chapters you want to export and wait for the downloads to finish.
2. Open the book's details and choose **Export** from the toolbar or the **•••** menu. The export button is a square with an upward arrow.
3. Choose **MMA** or **PDF** under **Export Format**.
4. Expand a chapter group and select the chapters you want. Tap the circle beside a group to select all of its downloaded chapters.
5. Tap **Export** and wait for **Export Complete**.
6. Tap **Share**, then choose **Save to Files** or another destination. Save or share the exported files before closing the export panel.

:::note[Chapters available for export]

Only fully downloaded chapters appear in the export list. If a chapter is missing, finish its download before exporting.

:::

You can also touch and hold a book in the downloads panel on **Home** and choose **Export**.
