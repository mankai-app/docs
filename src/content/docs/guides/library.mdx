**Home** brings together your collections and downloads. **Library** is where you discover content offered by your plugins.

## Save and find your favorites

1. Open a book's details and tap **Bookmark**.
2. Open **Home** and choose **Collections** in the source menu.
3. Search your saved titles or filter by status and source.
4. Pull down on the collection list to check for updates.

To remove a bookmark, open the book's details and tap **Remove**. Removing a bookmark and deleting a downloaded copy are separate actions.

## Find something to read

1. Open **Library** to see the lists offered by your plugins.
2. Open a title from a list, or use search if one of your plugins supports it.
3. Open the book's details and choose **Read**, **Continue**, or a chapter.

To return to a recently read book, open **Settings → History**. History includes books you have read even if you did not bookmark them.

## Download chapters

1. Open a book from a source that supports downloads and tap its download arrow.
2. In **Select Chapters**, expand a chapter group and select the chapters you want. Tap the circle beside a group to select all of its available chapters.
3. Tap **Download**.
4. Tap the download arrow on **Home** to check progress. A failed task has a retry button.

## Read offline and manage storage

1. Wait for the download to finish while the source is reachable.
2. Choose **Downloads** in the source menu on **Home** and open the book.
3. Open a downloaded chapter to read it offline.

Only the chapters you have downloaded are available offline. Bookmarking a title does not download its pages. To free storage, swipe a downloaded title in the downloads panel and confirm **Remove**. The download controls also let you cancel pending downloads or retry failed ones.
